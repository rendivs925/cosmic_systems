# **Module 2 Technical Specification: Hydrological & Thermal Erosion Simulation Engine**

## **1\. Mathematical Architecture & Physical Formulations**

Hydrological and thermal erosion sculpt macro terrain landforms (generated in Module 1\) into geomorphologically authentic landscapes with river networks, drainage basins, gullies, and debris slopes.

### **1.1 Particle-Based Hydraulic Erosion (Lagrangian Droplet Physics)**

Particle-based erosion simulates individual water drops traversing the heightmap continuous field \$H(\\mathbf{p})\$. A water droplet is modeled as a state vector:

\$\$\\mathbf{D} \= \\left( \\mathbf{p}, \\mathbf{v}, s, w, a \\right)\$\$  
where \$\\mathbf{p} \= (x, y)\$ is position, \$\\mathbf{v} \= (v\_x, v\_y)\$ is velocity, \$s\$ is sediment load, \$w\$ is water volume, and \$a\$ is droplet age.

#### **1\. Trajectory & Acceleration**

Terrain gradient \$\\mathbf{g} \= \\nabla H(\\mathbf{p}) \= \\left( \\frac{\\partial H}{\\partial x}, \\frac{\\partial H}{\\partial y} \\right)\$ is computed via bilinear interpolation of grid cell normals. Droplet velocity updates under gravity \$g\_{\\text{gravity}}\$ and inertia \$k\_{\\text{inertia}} \\in \[0.0, 1.0\]\$:

\$\$\\mathbf{v}\_{t+1} \= k\_{\\text{inertia}} \\mathbf{v}\_t \- (1 \- k\_{\\text{inertia}}) \\mathbf{g}\$\$

\$\$\\mathbf{p}\_{t+1} \= \\mathbf{p}\_t \+ \\mathbf{v}\_{t+1} \\cdot \\frac{\\mathbf{v}\_{t+1}}{\\Vert{}\\mathbf{v}\_{t+1}\\Vert{}}\$\$

#### **2\. Sediment Transport Capacity**

The maximum sediment mass \$C\$ a droplet can carry depends on speed \$\\Vert{}\\mathbf{v}\\Vert{}\$, slope angle \$\\alpha \= \\arcsin(\\Vert{}\\mathbf{g}\\Vert{})\$, and water volume \$w\$:

\$\$C \= w \\cdot \\Vert{}\\mathbf{v}\\Vert{} \\cdot \\sin(\\alpha) \\cdot k\_{\\text{capacity}}\$\$

#### **3\. Erosion & Deposition Dynamics**

> * **If \$s \> C\$ (Over-saturated):** Excess sediment deposits onto terrain at rate \$k\_{\\text{deposit}}\$:

\$\$\\Delta s \= (s \- C) \\cdot k\_{\\text{deposit}}\$\$

\$\$H(\\mathbf{p}) \\leftarrow H(\\mathbf{p}) \+ \\Delta s, \\quad s \\leftarrow s \- \\Delta s\$\$

> * **If \$s \< C\$ (Under-saturated):** Droplet dissolves substrate at rate \$k\_{\\text{erode}}\$, constrained by local soil hardness \$K\_{\\text{hard}}\$:

\$\$\\Delta s \= \\min\\left( (C \- s) \\cdot k\_{\\text{erode}} \\cdot (1.0 \- K\_{\\text{hard}}), \\, \\Delta H\_{\\text{max}} \\right)\$\$

\$\$H(\\mathbf{p}) \\leftarrow H(\\mathbf{p}) \- \\Delta s, \\quad s \\leftarrow s \+ \\Delta s\$\$

#### **4\. Evaporation & Mass Attenuation**

Water volume decreases per timestep via \$k\_{\\text{evap}}\$, while droplet speed decays due to bed friction \$k\_{\\text{friction}}\$:

\$\$w\_{t+1} \= w\_t \\cdot (1.0 \- k\_{\\text{evap}}), \\quad \\Vert{}\\mathbf{v}\_{t+1}\\Vert{} \= \\sqrt{\\Vert{}\\mathbf{v}\_{t+1}\\Vert{}^2 \\cdot (1.0 \- k\_{\\text{friction}})}\$\$

### **1.2 Grid-Based Shallow Water Equations (Eulerian Pipe Model)**

For large-scale river basin carving, Eulerian grid models simulate water column height \$W\$, flow fluxes \$\\mathbf{F} \= (f^L, f^R, f^T, f^B)\$, and velocity fields across grid coordinates \$(i, j)\$ derived from simplified 2D Saint-Venant shallow water equations.

                  Top Pipe (f^T)  
                       ▲  
                       │  
  Left Pipe (f^L) ◄───\[i, j\]───► Right Pipe (f^R)  
                       │  
                       ▼  
                 Bottom Pipe (f^B)

#### **1\. Outflow Flux Calculation**

Flux \$f\_d\$ toward neighbor \$d \\in \\{L, R, T, B\\}\$ updates based on hydrostatic pressure differential \$\\Delta H\_d\$:

\$\$\\Delta H\_d \= \\left( H\_{i,j} \+ W\_{i,j} \\right) \- \\left( H\_d \+ W\_d \\right)\$\$

\$\$f\_d^{t+1} \= \\max\\left( 0, \\, f\_d^t \+ \\Delta t \\cdot A \\cdot \\frac{g \\cdot \\Delta H\_d}{L} \\right)\$\$  
To enforce mass conservation, if total outflow exceeds available water volume \$W\_{i,j} \\cdot dx \\cdot dy\$, flux vectors scale down by factor \$K\_{\\text{scale}}\$:

\$\$K\_{\\text{scale}} \= \\min\\left(1.0, \\, \\frac{W\_{i,j} \\cdot dx \\cdot dy}{\\Delta t \\sum f\_d^{t+1}}\\right), \\quad f\_d^{t+1} \\leftarrow f\_d^{t+1} \\cdot K\_{\\text{scale}}\$\$

#### **2\. Water Column Update**

Net volume shift modifies grid cell depth \$W\_{i,j}\$:

\$\$\\Delta V \= \\Delta t \\left( \\sum f\_{\\text{in}} \- \\sum f\_{\\text{out}} \\right)\$\$

\$\$W\_{i,j}^{t+1} \= W\_{i,j}^t \+ \\frac{\\Delta V}{dx \\cdot dy}\$\$

### **1.3 Thermal Weathering & Talus Slopes (Angle of Repose)**

Thermal weathering models mechanical breakdown due to freeze-thaw cycles and gravity-driven mass wasting. When slope gradient between cell \$(i, j)\$ and neighbor \$(n\_x, n\_y)\$ exceeds critical **Angle of Repose** \$\\theta\_{\\text{repose}}\$, material cascades downhill to form stable scree/talus aprons.

\$\$\\Delta H\_{\\text{diff}} \= H\_{i,j} \- H\_{n\_x, n\_y}\$\$

\$\$\\Delta H\_{\\text{threshold}} \= L \\cdot \\tan(\\theta\_{\\text{repose}})\$\$  
Where \$L\$ is cell distance (\$1.0\$ for orthogonal, \$\\sqrt{2}\$ for diagonal neighbors).  
If \$\\Delta H\_{\\text{diff}} \> \\Delta H\_{\\text{threshold}}\$, unstable volume \$\\Delta M\$ collapses:

\$\$\\Delta M \= k\_{\\text{thermal}} \\cdot \\left( \\Delta H\_{\\text{diff}} \- \\Delta H\_{\\text{threshold}} \\right) \\cdot \\frac{\\Delta H\_{\\text{diff}}}{\\sum \\Delta H\_{\\text{exceed}}}\$\$

\$\$H\_{i,j} \\leftarrow H\_{i,j} \- \\Delta M, \\quad H\_{n\_x, n\_y} \\leftarrow H\_{n\_x, n\_y} \+ \\Delta M\$\$

## **2\. Execution Pipeline**

Simulation passes execute asynchronously using a ping-pong compute pipeline. Particle-based erosion carved details run locally, followed by grid-based flow passes, and concluded by thermal relaxation sweeps to stabilize cliffs.

## **3\. Production GLSL Compute Shader Implementation**

This compute shader performs GPU particle hydraulic erosion alongside dynamic stream flow tracking (which generates flow and sediment maps for biomes and shading in Modules 4 and 5).

OpenGL Shading Language  
\#version 450 core

layout(local\_size\_x \= 256, local\_size\_y \= 1, local\_size\_z \= 1\) in;

// \----------------------------------------------------------------------------  
// BINDINGS & BUFFERS  
// \----------------------------------------------------------------------------  
layout(r32f, binding \= 0\) uniform image2D u\_Heightmap;  
layout(rgba16f, binding \= 1\) uniform image2D u\_FlowAndSedimentMap; // R: Flow, G: Sediment, B: Hardness

struct Droplet {  
    vec2 position;  
    vec2 velocity;  
    float volume;  
    float sediment;  
};

layout(std430, binding \= 2\) buffer DropletBuffer {  
    Droplet droplets\[\];  
};

// Push Constants / Uniform Parameters  
layout(push\_constant) uniform ErosionParams {  
    int u\_MapResolution;      // Grid resolution (e.g., 2048\)  
    int u\_MaxLifetime;        // Max steps per droplet (e.g., 64\)  
    float u\_Inertia;          // Inertia coefficient (0.05 to 0.2)  
    float u\_CapacityFactor;   // Sediment capacity multiplier (4.0 to 8.0)  
    float u\_ErodeRate;        // Erosion rate (0.1 to 0.5)  
    float u\_DepositRate;      // Deposition rate (0.1 to 0.5)  
    float u\_EvapRate;         // Water evaporation rate (0.01 to 0.05)  
    float u\_Gravity;          // Gravitational acceleration (9.81)  
    float u\_MinSlope;         // Minimum slope threshold (0.01)  
} params;

// \----------------------------------------------------------------------------  
// TERRAIN GRADIENT UTILITIES  
// \----------------------------------------------------------------------------  
vec3 CalculateHeightAndGradient(vec2 pos) {  
    ivec2 coord \= ivec2(pos);  
    vec2 f \= fract(pos);

    // Bilinear height fetch  
    float h00 \= imageLoad(u\_Heightmap, coord).r;  
    float h10 \= imageLoad(u\_Heightmap, coord \+ ivec2(1, 0)).r;  
    float h01 \= imageLoad(u\_Heightmap, coord \+ ivec2(0, 1)).r;  
    float h11 \= imageLoad(u\_Heightmap, coord \+ ivec2(1, 1)).r;

    float gx \= mix(h10 \- h00, h11 \- h01, f.y);  
    float gy \= mix(h01 \- h00, h11 \- h10, f.x);

    float height \= mix(mix(h00, h10, f.x), mix(h01, h11, f.x), f.y);

    return vec3(gx, gy, height);  
}

void ModifyHeight(vec2 pos, float delta) {  
    ivec2 coord \= ivec2(pos);  
    vec2 f \= fract(pos);

    // Distribute height modifications across 4 neighboring texels via bilinear weights  
    float w00 \= (1.0 \- f.x) \* (1.0 \- f.y);  
    float w10 \= f.x \* (1.0 \- f.y);  
    float w01 \= (1.0 \- f.x) \* f.y;  
    float w11 \= f.x \* f.y;

    float currentH00 \= imageLoad(u\_Heightmap, coord).r;  
    float currentH10 \= imageLoad(u\_Heightmap, coord \+ ivec2(1, 0)).r;  
    float currentH01 \= imageLoad(u\_Heightmap, coord \+ ivec2(0, 1)).r;  
    float currentH11 \= imageLoad(u\_Heightmap, coord \+ ivec2(1, 1)).r;

    imageStore(u\_Heightmap, coord, vec4(currentH00 \+ delta \* w00));  
    imageStore(u\_Heightmap, coord \+ ivec2(1, 0), vec4(currentH10 \+ delta \* w10));  
    imageStore(u\_Heightmap, coord \+ ivec2(0, 1), vec4(currentH01 \+ delta \* w01));  
    imageStore(u\_Heightmap, coord \+ ivec2(1, 1), vec4(currentH11 \+ delta \* w11));  
}

// \----------------------------------------------------------------------------  
// MAIN DROPLET EROSION EVALUATION  
// \----------------------------------------------------------------------------  
void main() {  
    uint index \= gl\_GlobalInvocationID.x;  
    Droplet drop \= droplets\[index\];

    for (int step \= 0; step \< params.u\_MaxLifetime; \++step) {  
        ivec2 iPos \= ivec2(drop.position);

        // Boundary check  
        if (iPos.x \< 1 || iPos.x \>= params.u\_MapResolution \- 2 ||  
            iPos.y \< 1 || iPos.y \>= params.u\_MapResolution \- 2 || drop.volume \<= 0.0) {  
            break;  
        }

        vec3 gradAndHeight \= CalculateHeightAndGradient(drop.position);  
        vec2 grad \= gradAndHeight.xy;  
        float oldHeight \= gradAndHeight.z;

        // Compute new direction incorporating inertia  
        drop.velocity \= drop.velocity \* params.u\_Inertia \- grad \* (1.0 \- params.u\_Inertia);  
          
        float speed \= length(drop.velocity);  
        if (speed \== 0.0) {  
            // Pick arbitrary gradient direction if stalled  
            drop.velocity \= \-grad;  
            speed \= length(drop.velocity);  
        }

        if (speed \> 0.0) {  
            drop.velocity /= speed; // Normalize direction vector  
        }

        vec2 newPos \= drop.position \+ drop.velocity;  
        vec3 newGradAndHeight \= CalculateHeightAndGradient(newPos);  
        float newHeight \= newGradAndHeight.z;  
        float deltaHeight \= newHeight \- oldHeight;

        // Compute Sediment Capacity  
        float slope \= max(-deltaHeight, params.u\_MinSlope);  
        float capacity \= max(slope, params.u\_MinSlope) \* speed \* drop.volume \* params.u\_CapacityFactor;

        // Fetch local substrate properties from FlowAndSedimentMap  
        vec4 flowData \= imageLoad(u\_FlowAndSedimentMap, iPos);  
        float substrateHardness \= clamp(flowData.b, 0.0, 0.9);

        if (drop.sediment \> capacity || deltaHeight \> 0.0) {  
            // Deposit excess sediment  
            float amountToDeposit \= (deltaHeight \> 0.0) ?   
                min(deltaHeight, drop.sediment) :   
                (drop.sediment \- capacity) \* params.u\_DepositRate;

            drop.sediment \-= amountToDeposit;  
            ModifyHeight(drop.position, amountToDeposit);

            // Record deposition on sediment map (Green channel)  
            flowData.g \+= amountToDeposit \* 0.1;  
        } else {  
            // Erode underlying rock/soil  
            float amountToErode \= min((capacity \- drop.sediment) \* params.u\_ErodeRate \* (1.0 \- substrateHardness), \-deltaHeight);  
              
            drop.sediment \+= amountToErode;  
            ModifyHeight(drop.position, \-amountToErode);

            // Record stream flow accumulation on flow map (Red channel)  
            flowData.r \+= drop.volume \* 0.05;  
        }

        // Write updated metadata back to FlowAndSedimentMap  
        imageStore(u\_FlowAndSedimentMap, iPos, flowData);

        // Update droplet state parameters  
        drop.position \= newPos;  
        drop.speed \= sqrt(max(0.0, speed \* speed \+ deltaHeight \* params.u\_Gravity));  
        drop.volume \*= (1.0 \- params.u\_EvapRate);  
    }

    // Write back updated particle state  
    droplets\[index\] \= drop;  
}

## **4\. Parameter Specification Table**

| Parameter Name | Symbol | Range / Units | Recommended Default | Physical Effect / Description |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **Inertia Coefficient** | \$k\_{\\text{inertia}}\$ | \$0.0 \- 0.3\$ | \$0.1\$ | Momentum persistence. Higher values produce sweeping wide river bends; lower values follow sharp local dips. |
| **Capacity Factor** | \$k\_{\\text{capacity}}\$ | \$2.0 \- 12.0\$ | \$6.0\$ | Base capacity scaling multiplier. Controls total sediment mass carried per water volume unit. |
| **Erosion Rate** | \$k\_{\\text{erode}}\$ | \$0.05 \- 0.5\$ | \$0.25\$ | Substrate detachment rate. High values carve deep V-shaped drainage gullies. |
| **Deposition Rate** | \$k\_{\\text{deposit}}\$ | \$0.05 \- 0.5\$ | \$0.15\$ | Rate of sediment drop-off. High values build expansive alluvial fans at canyon mouths. |
| **Evaporation Rate** | \$k\_{\\text{evap}}\$ | \$0.005 \- 0.05\$ | \$0.02\$ | Volumetric water loss per step. Controls maximum stream length before drying up. |
| **Angle of Repose** | \$\\theta\_{\\text{repose}}\$ | \$28^\\circ \- 42^\\circ\$ | \$35^\\circ\$ | Critical slope angle threshold. Controls maximum stable cliff angle before talus collapse occurs. |
| **Thermal Rate** | \$k\_{\\text{thermal}}\$ | \$0.1 \- 0.6\$ | \$0.3\$ | Speed of thermal rock breakdown during talus relaxation passes. |
