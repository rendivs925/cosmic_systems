# Unified Physics Found in the Quran

## The One-Fluid Universe

## Lecture 1: The Verse That Did Not Fit

> وَهُوَ ٱلَّذِي خَلَقَ ٱلَّيْلَ وَٱلنَّهَارَ وَٱلشَّمْسَ وَٱلْقَمَرَۖ كُلٌّ فِي فَلَكٍ يَسْبَحُونَ
>
> "And He is the One Who created the day and the night, the sun and the moon, each travelling in an orbit." (Quran 21:33)

This verse has troubled observers for fourteen centuries. It describes the sun and the moon as swimming in an orbit, and for most of that time no one could explain the word. Physics assumed that space was empty, a void, a stage on which objects moved but which itself had no properties. Nothing swims through nothing. The verse was filed away as poetry.

The claim of this article is that the text was pointing at a real property of the universe, one that physics has only recently begun to describe. The word was not chosen carelessly, and the assumption it contradicts was not correct.

## Lecture 2: The Word Yasbahoon

The Arabic verb is يسبحون, *yasbahoon*, from the root س-ب-ح. The primary sense of that root is to swim, to move through a fluid medium by propulsion against it. The same root gives the language its word for swimming, and the Quran uses it for creatures moving through water and for celestial bodies alike. In 21:33 and again in 36:40 the sun and the moon, and indeed all bodies, swim:

> لَا ٱلشَّمْسُ يَنۢبَغِي لَهَآ أَن تُدْرِكَ ٱلْقَمَرَ وَلَا ٱلَّيْلُ سَابِقُ ٱلنَّهَارِۚ وَكُلٌّ فِي فَلَكٍ يَسْبَحُونَ
>
> "It is not for the sun to catch up with the moon, nor does the night outrun the day. Each is travelling in an orbit of their own." (Quran 36:40)

The root is precise. A body that travels across empty void does not swim. The verb demands a medium, and the grammar extends the claim to all heavenly bodies, since the plural verb is applied to subjects that are grammatically only two. The text commits itself to a universe in which celestial bodies move within a substance.

This is the puzzle that the rest of this article addresses.

## Lecture 3: What Is Space?

Modern physics calls space nothing, and yet space is observed to bend, to ripple, and to be dragged. These are properties, and properties belong to substances.

Einstein showed in 1915 that gravity is the geometry of spacetime, and within four years Eddington measured starlight bending around the sun during an eclipse, confirming that space itself curves. In 2011 the Gravity Probe B mission, led by Everitt, measured frame dragging, the slow pulling of space around a rotating body, and confirmed that moving mass carries the surrounding medium along with it. In 2015 the LIGO and Virgo observatories detected gravitational waves, ripples in space itself, traveling outward from colliding black holes.

If space were truly empty, none of these measurements would be possible. An empty void has nothing to bend, nothing to ripple, and nothing to drag. Every measured property of space is a property of a medium. The conclusion is that space is the medium, and every body in it moves through that medium. We have been swimming in it the whole time.

## Lecture 4: Why Does Everything Keep Moving?

Newton's first law states that an object in motion stays in motion unless acted upon by a force. The law works, but it was always unexplained. Why should motion persist forever?

A superfluid offers the answer. A superfluid has zero viscosity, which means a body moving through it never slows down from friction. The medium does not resist. An object swims through a superfluid and keeps its speed indefinitely.

Inertia is then not a mysterious property of bodies. It is the frictionlessness of the medium in which they move. The marble rolls forever because the table is frictionless, and the universe, on this account, is frictionless as well. Newton's first law is not an axiom handed down from nowhere. It is a consequence of the medium's nature.

## Lecture 5: The Hundred Year War

General relativity and quantum mechanics contradict each other, and reconciling them has been the central problem of theoretical physics for a century. General relativity describes the large scale as smooth and deterministic. Quantum mechanics describes the small scale as discrete and probabilistic. They do not agree on what space is, what time is, or what a particle is.

The fluid picture dissolves the conflict by construction. General relativity is the large scale behavior of a quantum fluid, the way a calm lake has a smooth surface, and quantum mechanics is the small scale structure of that same fluid, the way the same lake is made of discrete molecules. The two descriptions do not fight because they are two views of one substance.

Physics has been walking toward this image for decades. Unruh showed in 1981 that flowing fluids reproduce the geometry of curved spacetime, including black hole horizons, for sound waves. Jacobson showed in 1995 that Einstein's equations emerge from the laws of thermodynamics, which are the laws of flow and heat. Volovik proposed in 2003 that the vacuum is a superfluid, and he worked out the consequences in his book The Universe in a Helium Droplet. Verlinde argued in 2011 that gravity is an entropic force, a large scale consequence of microscopic order. They are not two theories. They are one theory, seen at two distances.

## Lecture 6: Why Is Gravity So Weak?

Gravity is about ten to the thirty sixth power weaker than electromagnetism. A small magnet can lift a paperclip against the entire pull of the Earth. Physics has treated this enormous gap as a coincidence, an unexplained fact about the universe.

The fluid picture explains it. Gravity is not a fundamental force. It is the backpressure of the medium, an emergent effect of the fluid's flow. Emergent forces are weaker than the forces of the parts from which they emerge, because they are averages, large scale residues of much stronger microscopic interactions. The weakness of gravity is then not a coincidence. It is exactly what should be expected of a force that is a property of the whole ocean rather than of its drops.

Gravity is the whisper of the ocean, faint because it is the entire sea moving at once.

## Lecture 7: The Absurd Vacuum

Quantum physics predicts that empty space should carry enormous energy, so much that it would tear the universe apart. The prediction misses the observed value by a factor of ten to the one hundred twentieth power, which is the worst discrepancy in the history of science. Physicists have called it the most embarrassing prediction in the field.

The fluid picture offers a natural resolution. A superfluid at its ground state is calm. Its energy is near zero because it is at rest, coherent, and without internal friction. If the vacuum is a superfluid, then its near zero energy is not an accident. It is the stillness of the fluid.

The absurd number came from treating the vacuum as a storm of infinite independent particles. The fluid picture treats it as a quiet medium, and the quiet is the very fact that physics had to explain. The fluid's stillness is the number physics got wrong.

## Lecture 8: Why Do Galaxies Hold Together?

In the 1970s Rubin measured how fast galaxies rotate, and the measurements showed that galaxies spin too quickly for their visible matter to hold them together. Something unseen was providing the extra pull. Physics responded by postulating dark matter, an invisible substance that has been searched for ever since and never found.

The fluid picture gives another account. A medium can carry texture, knots, defects, and turbulence, and those structures produce gravitational effects without adding any new substance. The extra pull that holds galaxies together can be the structure of the fluid itself, the knots in the one substance we already have.

We looked for a new substance and missed the knots in the one we already have.

## Lecture 9: Why Is Space Expanding Faster?

In 1998 Riess and others measured the distances to distant supernovae and found that the expansion of the universe is accelerating. Something was pushing space apart, and physics responded by postulating dark energy, another invisible ingredient, this one with a negative pressure that drives expansion.

The fluid picture explains the acceleration as the medium's intrinsic tension. A stretched membrane naturally wants to expand, and a fluid under tension behaves the same way. The expansion of the universe is then a property of the medium itself, not an added ingredient. The Quran describes the heaven as built and expanding:

> وَٱلسَّمَآءَ بَنَيْنَٰهَا بِأَيْيْدٍ وَإِنَّا لَمُوسِعُونَ
>
> "We built the universe with great might, and We are certainly expanding it." (Quran 51:47)

The phrase is exact. The universe is not being pushed. The medium is breathing outward.

## Lecture 10: Why Is Quantum Weird?

Quantum mechanics accepts as brute facts several things that seem to violate common sense. A particle behaves as a wave. Spin comes only in fixed sizes. Energy is exchanged only in discrete steps.

Superfluid helium shows that this discreteness is a property of fluids. Superfluids form whirlpools, and those whirlpools only spin in fixed sizes. The circulation is quantized, a real measured fact of laboratory physics, and the same quantization appears in the clouds of cold atoms called Bose-Einstein condensates, studied since 1995.

If particles are structures in a superfluid vacuum, then quantum discreteness is not a mystery. It is the whirlpool structure of the fluid, a substance that only knows how to spin in whole turns. The quantum world is not weird. It is a fluid with a strict sense of rotation.

## Lecture 11: Black Holes

Black holes have been treated as singularities, points where physics itself breaks down. Hawking and Bekenstein showed in the 1970s that black holes carry entropy and radiate, and their work produced a paradox that has stood for fifty years: if a black hole radiates away and disappears, what happens to the information that fell in?

The fluid picture resolves both the singularity and the paradox. A black hole is a vortex, a whirlpool in the medium whose current exceeds the speed of light. Nothing falls into a hole in nothing. Matter is caught in a current it cannot swim against, and it is carried, reorganized, and ultimately returned through radiation, the way water that falls into a drain returns as weather.

Laboratory physics has already made the analogy concrete. Unruh's flowing fluid experiments and the atom cloud analogs recreate black hole horizons in the lab, and a rotating superfluid forms the same structure at a small scale. Penrose proved that singularities must exist under the classical assumptions, but the fluid picture does not need a singularity at all, only a current too strong to escape. A black hole is a drain in the ocean of space, and the ocean keeps its own.

## Lecture 12: Why Does Nothing Fit Together?

Modern physics is a patchwork. It has separate forces, invisible substances, unexplained constants, and contradictory frames. The seams are everywhere, and the discipline has learned to live with them.

The Quran proposes a test. In Surah Al-Mulk, verses three and four, it says that the creation of the Most Compassionate shows no imperfection, and it invites the reader to look again and again, returning the gaze until it is certain:

> ٱلَّذِي خَلَقَ سَبۡعَ سَمَٰوَٰتٖ طِبَاقٗاۖ مَّا تَرَىٰ فِي خَلۡقِ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنِ مِن تَفَٰوُتٖۖ فَٱرۡجِعِ ٱلۡبَصَرَ هَلۡ تَرَىٰ مِن فُطُورٖ
>
> "He is the One Who created seven heavens, one above the other. You will never see any imperfection in the creation of the Most Compassionate. So look again: do you see any flaws?"
>
> ثُمَّ ٱرۡجِعِ ٱلۡبَصَرَ كَرَّتَيۡنِ يَنقَلِبۡ إِلَيۡكَ ٱلۡبَصَرُ خَاسِئٗا وَهُوَ حَسِيرٞ
>
> "Then look again and again, your sight will return frustrated and weary." (Quran 67:3-4)

The verse is a claim about the universe: there is no seam in creation.

The fluid picture is the physics of that claim. One substance, one law, many forms. The seams are not in the universe. They are in the model that assumed space was empty and then had to invent ingredients to repair the damage. The inconsistency was never in creation. It was in our model.

## Lecture 13: The Honest Test

None of this proves the Quran, and this article does not claim it does. The relationship runs in the opposite direction. The text raised precise questions fourteen centuries before physics had the tools to answer them, and physics has independently walked toward the same image. That convergence is worth studying, but it is not proof of anything, and it should not be treated as such.

What matters is that the claims in this article are testable. Each one has an experiment that would confirm it or end it. The claim that space is a medium can be tested by measuring whether gravitational waves disperse as they travel. The claim that orbits are carried flow can be tested by measuring frame dragging with greater precision. The claim that dark energy is the medium's tension can be tested by measuring whether its behavior changes over time. The claim that quantum discreteness is the fluid's quantization can be tested in the vortex structures of superfluids. If the measurements agree, the picture is strengthened. If they do not, the picture dies.

That is the requirement of a scientific claim, and this article accepts it. We do not ask you to believe us. We ask you to test it, and to let the universe decide.
